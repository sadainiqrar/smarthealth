# Booking and payment policies

## Cancellation policy and late cancellation fee

You can cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. If you cancel later
than that, a late cancellation fee of 50 percent of the visit price may be charged, because the
time could have been offered to someone on the waitlist. Cancellations because your clinician
is unavailable are never charged.

## Missed appointments (no-shows)

If you do not arrive and have not cancelled, the appointment is recorded as missed and you
receive a note inviting you to rebook. After three missed appointments in twelve months we may
ask you to pay in advance for future bookings.

## Payment and the card hold

When you book, a hold for the visit price is placed on your card; nothing is charged yet. The
charge is taken after your visit. If the appointment is cancelled, the hold is released, and
if you were already charged, the charge is refunded — usually within five working days.

## Privacy

Your records are seen only by you, by clinicians involved in your care, and by staff who need
them to run your appointments. Front-desk staff see your bookings, not your diagnoses or
results. You can ask for a copy of your records at any time.
