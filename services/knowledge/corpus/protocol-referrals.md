# Clinical referral protocols

For clinicians and front-desk staff. Not shown to patients.

## Physiotherapy referral process

Patients may self-book physiotherapy for musculoskeletal pain. A clinician referral is required
after surgery, after a fracture, for neurological rehabilitation, and for patients under 16.
The referring clinician records the reason and any precautions in the clinical note, places a
physiotherapy test order, and front desk books the first appointment within ten working days.

## Specialist referral

A GP refers to a specialist by placing an order naming the specialty and the question to be
answered. Urgent referrals (suspected cancer, rapidly worsening symptoms) must be booked within
two weeks; front desk escalates to the department lead if no slot is free.

## Reviewing results

The ordering clinician reviews every result within two working days of it arriving, marks it
reviewed, and contacts the patient if action is needed. An abnormal result that cannot be
acted on the same day is handed over to the clinician on duty.
