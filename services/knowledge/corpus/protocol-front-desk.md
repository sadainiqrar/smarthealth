# Front desk procedures

For front-desk staff. Not shown to patients.

## Registering a walk-in patient

Check photo identification, search for an existing record by name and date of birth before
creating one (duplicates split a patient's history), record a phone number or email for
reminders, and give the patient their medical record number (MRN).

## Checking a patient in

Check the patient in at arrival, not when they are called: Average Wait Time is measured from
check-in to being seen. A patient arriving more than fifteen minutes after the start of their
slot may be asked to rebook if the clinician cannot fit them in.

## When a clinician is unavailable

If a clinician becomes unavailable, their future appointments are cancelled automatically with
the reason "clinician unavailable" and patients are notified. Offer each patient the next free
time with another clinician of the same specialty, or the waitlist.
