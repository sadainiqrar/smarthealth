# MediNova specialties

This guide describes what each MediNova specialty looks after, so you can choose who to
book. It is not a diagnosis. If you are unsure, book General Practice: a GP can assess you
and refer you on. If you have chest pain, signs of a stroke or severe bleeding, do not book —
see the urgent care guide.

## Cardiology

Cardiology looks after the heart and blood vessels: high blood pressure, palpitations or an
irregular heartbeat, breathlessness or chest tightness on exertion, high cholesterol, heart
failure, and follow-up after a heart attack or heart surgery. A cardiologist may arrange an
ECG, an echocardiogram (heart ultrasound) or an exercise test.

## Dermatology

Dermatology treats the skin, hair and nails: eczema, psoriasis, acne, rashes that do not
settle, hair loss, nail infections, and moles that have changed shape, colour or size. Bring
photos if a rash comes and goes.

## Endocrinology

Endocrinology deals with hormones and metabolism: diabetes and its control (HbA1c), thyroid
problems (an underactive or overactive thyroid), osteoporosis, and adrenal or pituitary
disorders. Many endocrinology appointments start with blood tests.

## Gastroenterology

Gastroenterology covers the digestive system: persistent heartburn or reflux, abdominal pain,
a change in bowel habit, blood in the stool, coeliac disease, irritable bowel syndrome, liver
problems such as fatty liver, and colonoscopy or endoscopy.

## General Practice

General Practice is the place to start for most new problems, for long-term conditions, for
vaccinations and repeat prescriptions, for sick notes, and whenever you are not sure which
specialist you need. A GP coordinates referrals to every other specialty.

## Neurology

Neurology looks after the brain, spinal cord and nerves: recurring or severe headaches and
migraine, seizures, numbness or tingling, tremor, problems with balance or memory, and
multiple sclerosis or Parkinson's disease follow-up.

## Orthopaedics

Orthopaedics treats bones, joints, ligaments and tendons: joint pain and arthritis in the
knee, hip or shoulder, sports injuries, back problems that have not improved with
physiotherapy, and fractures after the emergency stage.

## Paediatrics

Paediatrics cares for babies, children and teenagers up to 16: development and growth checks,
childhood asthma and allergies, recurring infections, and feeding concerns. A parent or
guardian must attend with a child under 16.

## Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy helps you move and recover: back and neck pain, rehabilitation after an injury
or operation, joint stiffness, and exercise programmes. Physiotherapy can be booked directly
for muscle and joint problems; some conditions need a referral (see the referral protocol).

## Ear, Nose and Throat (ENT)

ENT treats the ears, nose and throat: hearing loss, ear infections, tinnitus, sinus problems,
nosebleeds, tonsillitis, and hoarseness lasting more than three weeks.
