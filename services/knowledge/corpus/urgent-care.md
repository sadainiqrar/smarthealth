# When it cannot wait

Do not book an appointment for any of the following. Call the emergency number or go to the
nearest emergency department now.

## Chest pain

Chest pain or pressure that spreads to the arm, jaw or back, or comes with breathlessness,
sweating or sickness, may be a heart attack. Call the emergency number immediately.

## Signs of a stroke

Use FAST: Face drooping on one side, Arm weakness, Speech slurred or strange, Time to call the
emergency number. Sudden severe headache, sudden confusion or loss of vision are also
emergencies.

## Severe bleeding, breathing difficulty, or collapse

Bleeding that will not stop with pressure, severe difficulty breathing, choking, a seizure
lasting more than five minutes, or someone who has collapsed and will not wake: call the
emergency number.

## Thoughts of self-harm

If you are thinking about harming yourself, call the emergency number or a crisis line now.
You will be listened to.

## Same-day but not an emergency

A high temperature that will not come down, a urinary infection, a minor injury or a child who
is unwell but alert: call the front desk for a same-day General Practice appointment.
