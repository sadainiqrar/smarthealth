# Preparing for a scan or X-ray

## MRI scan

An MRI uses a strong magnet. Tell us before your appointment if you have a pacemaker, a
cochlear implant, any metal fragments in your body, or you might be pregnant. Remove jewellery,
watches and hair clips before the scan; you will be offered a locker. The scan is noisy and
takes 20 to 45 minutes; earplugs are provided. If you are claustrophobic, tell us when you
book.

## MRI with contrast

Some MRI scans use a contrast dye injected into a vein. You may be asked for a recent kidney
blood test (creatinine) beforehand. Eat and drink normally unless told otherwise. Tell us if
you have had a reaction to contrast before, or if you are breastfeeding.

## Abdominal ultrasound

For an ultrasound of the abdomen, do not eat for six hours beforehand so the gallbladder can
be seen. You may drink water. For a pelvic ultrasound, drink about a litre of water an hour
before and do not empty your bladder: a full bladder makes the scan clearer.

## CT scan

A CT scan uses X-rays. If contrast is planned you may be asked not to eat for four hours
before. Tell us if you have diabetes and take metformin, have kidney problems, or might be
pregnant.

## X-ray

Most X-rays need no preparation. You may be asked to change into a gown and remove metal
objects. Tell the radiographer if you might be pregnant.
