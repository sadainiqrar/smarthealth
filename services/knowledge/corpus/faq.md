# Frequently asked questions

## How do I book an appointment?

Book through the patient portal or the MediNova assistant, or call the front desk. Choose a
specialty, then a clinician and a free time. Your booking is confirmed once it shows as
confirmed — usually within seconds — and you receive a confirmation email.

## How do I reschedule or move my appointment?

Use the portal or the assistant to move it to another free time. Your new appointment is
booked first and the old one is released only once the new one is confirmed, so you never
end up with no appointment.

## What is the waitlist?

If no time suits you, join the waitlist for a clinician and a window of time. When a matching
appointment is cancelled, the waitlist is offered it automatically, oldest request first, and
you are booked if it is still free.

## What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring photo identification, your appointment confirmation, a list of the medicines you take,
and any letters or results from other clinics. Arrive ten minutes early to check in at the
front desk.

## Can I get an interpreter?

Yes. Ask for an interpreter when you book, giving the language you need. Interpreting is free
for patients.

## Is there parking?

Each clinic has limited patient parking; the first two hours are free. Riverside and North
clinics are also on bus routes from the city centre.

## When will I get my results?

Most results reach your clinician within two working days, scans within five. Your clinician
reviews them and contacts you if you need to act; you can also see results you uploaded in the
portal.
